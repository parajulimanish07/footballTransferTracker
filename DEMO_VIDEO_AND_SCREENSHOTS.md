## 🎬 90-Second Demo Video Script

**Title:** *AI-Powered Football Transfer Intelligence Platform Overview*  
**Target Duration:** 90 seconds  

### **Scene 1: Introduction & Global Transfer Feed (0:00 – 0:25)**
- **Visual:** Smooth scroll down `/dashboard` showing live transfer cards.
- **Voiceover:** 
  > *"Welcome to the Football Transfer Intelligence Platform—an AI-powered application designed to cut through transfer window noise, clickbait, and duplicate rumors."*
- **Action:** Click on the **Explainable AI Signals** button on a transfer card to open the modal showing TF-IDF n-gram feature breakdown.

### **Scene 2: Clause NLP & Entity Resolution (0:25 – 0:45)**
- **Visual:** Highlight the `Trafford ➔ Leeds United` movement card.
- **Voiceover:** 
  > *"Using clause-level NLP sentence parsing and a 5-tier fallback entity catalogue, the platform accurately extracts player origin and destination clubs from complex multi-rumor headlines, associating authentic crest logos without cross-sentence contamination."*

### **Scene 3: Grounded Hybrid RAG Assistant (0:45 – 1:10)**
- **Visual:** Open the AI Assistant drawer in the bottom right corner and type: *"What is the latest update on Spurs?"*
- **Voiceover:** 
  > *"Our Grounded RAG assistant combines 40% semantic vector search with keyword matching, source authority scoring, and prompt injection defense to deliver citation-backed answers."*

### **Scene 4: Admin Telemetry & Wrap Up (1:10 – 1:30)**
- **Visual:** Switch to `/admin/entities` dashboard and show the 145/145 passing unit test badge on GitHub.
- **Voiceover:** 
  > *"Backed by 145 passing unit tests, zero TypeScript errors, and Next.js 15 App Router architecture, the system maintains a fast local entity snapshot for supported leagues. Check out the full source code on GitHub!"*
- **End Card:** GitHub Repository URL & Links.
